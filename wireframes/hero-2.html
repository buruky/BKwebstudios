Layers (back to front) and parallax

Mouse move shifts each layer by a different amount, eased with a spring. Off on touch devices and for reduced motion.

Load sequence

0ms: photo visible, fully clear, "local" already behind ridge → 200ms: fog drifts in from the left edge to the seam (1100ms, strong ease-in-out) → 900ms: frame line draws itself around the perimeter (900ms) → 1200ms: small text + switcher + buttons fade up with 60ms stagger (500ms, ease-out). Reduced motion: everything just appears, fog included.
