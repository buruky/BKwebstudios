Hero 4 · "Retro" wireframe (v2, after the Poolsuite-style reference)

Grey-box only, drawn at 1920 × 920. Classic Mac OS 7 / 1-bit, not a modern desktop: flat pale page, boxed chips in the corners, one player window in the middle, square app tiles along the bottom. The one idea: switching style = changing the channel. The screen fills with static between styles. Numbers map to the notes below.

Layers (back to front) and motion

Old-computer motion: instant or stepped, no soft easing, no blur. Static stops and everything appears at once for reduced motion.

1 · Pageflat pale colorfixed
2 · Chips + tilesbrand, price, navfixed
3 · Windowframe, panels, buttons, switcherfixed
4 · Staticnoise canvas, ~12 fpsanimated
5 · Title cardheadline over the staticfixed

Load sequence (all done by ~0.5s)

0ms: page, chips and tiles → 50ms: the window draws in (outline first, then fills) → 150ms: the screen turns on as full static with a quick white line, like an old TV warming up → 350ms: the title card appears over it. Reduced motion: everything just appears, static still.
