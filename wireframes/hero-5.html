Hero 5 · "Collage" wireframe (v1, after the Plura portfolio deck)

Grey-box only, drawn at 1920 × 920. A dark page made up like a portfolio slide, turned up to a layered collage. The one idea: a huge marble bust torn into strips, held together by one ember strip of tape that carries the key word "small". Giant thin caps split across two corners (like PART TWO / PACKAGING), tiny tracked labels, vertical text, a contact grid of client sites. Dark grey = the dune page; light grey = the bust and type. Numbers map to the notes below.

Notes

  1. 1Label row, tiny caps with wide tracking like "Portfolio of Plura | 01.Branding": name + "05. Collage" left, nav centre, "Built by Buruk in Seattle" right (in the reference's "Created and Designed by" spot). Hairline under it.
  2. 2Headline, part 1: "CUSTOM WEBSITES / FOR" in giant thin caps (a light geometric sans, like the reference), top-left corner. A torn stone-texture strip runs behind it.
  3. 3The bust: one Met Open Access marble head (pick below), greyscale + grain, huge, running off the top, right and bottom. Cut into 4 torn horizontal strips, each nudged a few px sideways, so it reads as a collage.
  4. 4Ember tape across the eyes: a torn strip of ember paper (the only color on the page) with "small" set in big italic serif. It crosses from the page onto the bust, joining the headline to the image.
  5. 5Description, small body text, as in the reference's PROJECTS slide.

  7. 7Contact grid of your client sites (like their RESEARCH grid): 6 small greyscale tiles, color on hover, the whole grid jumps to Work. No words.
  8. 8Headline, part 2: "BUSINESSES" giant thin caps in the bottom-right corner, laid over the bust's chest (like PART TWO / PACKAGING split across corners).
  9. 9Switcher as a slide pager "‹ 05 / 05 COLLAGE ›", with the shared hint line under it.
  10. 10Vertical text up the left edge: "Seattle · Websites from $750". The photo credit runs down the right edge, tiny.
  11. 11Motion: on load the bust strips slide in from alternating sides and lock into place, then the ember tape slaps on (~1.2s total). Mouse: each strip shifts at its own depth. Scroll: the strips drift apart as the sheet rises.
